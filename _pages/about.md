My work focuses on **LLM safety**, **recommender systems** and **multimodal emotion recognition**. I currently lead generative AI research at [Kompanion](https://www.kompanioncare.com/), where we build digital therapeutics for brain health, after a PhD on multimodal and unsupervised learning at CY Cergy Paris University.

In parallel, I'm driven by a passion for using AI to assist disabled individuals. My personal projects aim to make AI a transformative force in their daily lives, creating inclusive and accessible solutions.

I also write. [Cracking AI](/blog/) is where I take generative models apart and rebuild them from scratch, starting with a five-part series on Stable Diffusion.
