I lead AI research at [Kompanion](https://www.kompanioncare.com/), where we build digital therapeutics for brain health. My current research focuses on **LLM safety** and **hallucination mitigation**, building on a PhD in multimodal and unsupervised learning applied to recommender systems.

In parallel, I'm driven by a passion for using multimodal AI to support investment decisions: combining market data, text and other signals to act in real time, with proper risk management at the core.

I also teach and write. [Cracking AI](/blog/) is my technical blog about AI: each post builds the intuition, explains the ideas properly, and comes with a hands-on implementation you can run yourself.
